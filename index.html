CRISPOT Privacy Policy

Cellagen Technology — CRISPOT mobile application (com.crispot.crispot)

Effective 22 September 2026 · Last updated 22 September 2026

Cellagen Technology does not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information or your test results. Everything the app handles — including every diagnostic result — is created and kept on your own device. CRISPOT has no user accounts, no advertising, and no tracking, and we operate no server that receives your data.

One exception, set out in full in section 6: the Google ML Kit component that decodes reagent QR codes sends Google limited technical data about itself, such as your device model and how long a scan took. It does not send your test results, images from your camera, or anything that identifies you.

1. Who we are

CRISPOT is developed and published by Cellagen Technology ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the CRISPOT mobile application for Android and iOS handles information. It applies to the app only.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or have any access to:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account or contact information
  • Your location, or any approximate or precise position data
  • Photographs, video, or audio
  • Your test results, measurements, or any other health information
  • Advertising identifiers. CRISPOT does not use an advertising ID and contains no advertising software
  • Analytics about how you use the app, or any record of your activity in it
  • Crash reports (see section 6 for the technical diagnostics that the Google QR-scanning component sends to Google)
  • Contacts, calendar, files, messages, or browsing activity

We operate no servers and no accounts, so there is no system of ours for your information to reach. The app does hold network access, which the Google ML Kit component used for QR-code scanning requires; section 6 explains what that component sends and to whom.

3. Information stored on your device

To do its job, CRISPOT writes the following to private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export and share it (see section 5).

WhatWhy
Test results received from CRISPOT hardware — readings, channel data, run times, and the assay configuration a run used So you can review, filter, and export past results
Identifiers reported by your CRISPOT hardware, such as its serial number and Bluetooth device name To label results and to reconnect to a device you have used before
App settings, including your chosen language and any organization name or report title you enter for PDF reports To remember your preferences and to render report headers
Generated PDF and CSV export files So an export can be shared or re-opened without regenerating it

This information is held in the app's private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read.

4. Device permissions and why they are needed

PermissionPurpose
Bluetooth (scan and connect) To find and communicate with CRISPOT diagnostic hardware over Bluetooth Low Energy. This is the app's core function.
Location (Android 11 and earlier only) The app does not use, derive, store, or transmit your location. Versions of Android before 12 withhold all Bluetooth scan results unless a location permission has been granted, so the permission is declared solely to make Bluetooth scanning work on those older devices. On Android 12 and later the app declares Bluetooth scanning as never being used to derive location.
Camera To scan reagent QR codes. Decoding happens entirely on your device, and no image or video is recorded, saved, or transmitted. See section 6 for the technical diagnostics the decoding component reports to Google.
Notifications and foreground service A diagnostic run can take around 30 minutes, and the hardware sends its result only once. A foreground service with a visible notification keeps the Bluetooth connection alive so a result is not lost if the screen turns off or you switch apps.

5. Exporting and sharing your results

CRISPOT can generate PDF reports and CSV files from your saved results. These are produced on your device. They are shared only when you choose to share them — when you tap Share or Print and select a destination, your device's own sharing system passes the file to the app you picked, such as email or a file manager.

We are not a party to that transfer and never receive a copy. Once you send a file to another app or service, that app or service's own privacy policy governs what happens to it.

6. Third-party components

CRISPOT contains no advertising networks, no attribution or marketing software, and no third-party crash-reporting service. Most of the libraries it is built from — for Bluetooth communication, PDF generation, and file sharing — run entirely on your device and send nothing anywhere.

One component is different and we want to be explicit about it. Reagent QR codes are decoded by Google ML Kit. The decoding itself happens entirely on your device: no image, video, or scan content is uploaded. However, by Google's own documentation, ML Kit reports limited technical diagnostics back to Google, namely:

  • Device information, such as manufacturer, model, operating system version and build
  • Performance metrics for the component, such as how long an operation took

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not passed by Google to third parties. Google does not currently provide a way for developers to switch this reporting off. It contains none of your test results, none of your camera content, and nothing that identifies you personally. Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; their description of what ML Kit collects is published at developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

This is the only data any part of CRISPOT sends off your device on its own. Cellagen Technology does not receive it and has no access to it.

7. Health information

Diagnostic test results may constitute health information. CRISPOT treats them accordingly: results are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, are never transmitted to us, and are never used for any purpose beyond displaying and exporting them at your request. You decide whether a result is ever shared, and with whom.

8. Retaining and deleting your information

Because we never receive your information, we hold nothing to retain or delete. Information stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You are in full control:

  • Delete individual results, or a selection of results, from the Results screen in the app.
  • Clear all app data through your device's system settings for CRISPOT.
  • Uninstall CRISPOT, which removes all results, settings, and exported files it created.

Files you have already exported and shared elsewhere are outside the app's control and must be removed wherever you sent them.

9. Security

Information is stored in app-private storage protected by the operating system's own application sandbox and, on a device with a screen lock, by that device's encryption. Because your results are never transmitted to us and we run no server that holds them, there is no database of your data to be breached. Please protect your device with a screen lock and keep its operating system up to date.

10. Children's privacy

CRISPOT is a professional diagnostic tool intended for adult users operating CRISPOT hardware. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play Data safety disclosure. Please review this page periodically.
